# Social-ties maps on real borders

## In short (for people)

The social-ties maps were grown on the toys' own neighbour links, which are partly guessed from block-group centres. Checked on real Census borders, some of their districts fall into pieces: 39 of 44 county maps and 42 ZIP maps have at least one district in more than one piece, some with large parts tens of kilometres away.

Both maps were redrawn on real borders with the same method and settings (Connecticut has only the ZIP map). In the 43 states with both, districts left in pieces: county maps 0, ZIP maps 0. Democratic seats with 2020 votes, added up over those states: county map 223 before, 226 now; ZIP map 229 before, 229 now; neutral median 223. The old files are kept; the new ones end in `.census.json`.
